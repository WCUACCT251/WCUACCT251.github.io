Adjusting the accounts

Practice quiz on accrual vs. cash basis, the four types of adjusting entries, and the adjusted trial balance.

How this quiz works:

  • 16 multiple-choice questions, one best answer each.
  • Check your answers any time; you’ll see your score and an explanation for every question.
  • Retake it as often as you like. Nothing is recorded or graded.

3Question 3: Which statement about accrual-basis and cash-basis accounting is true?
