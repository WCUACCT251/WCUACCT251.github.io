Accounting cycle review

Journalize and post a full month of activity for Bryson City Bike Works, then prove it out with a trial balance.

Three steps:

  • Journalize 15 November transactions in the general journal.
  • Post every entry to the T-accounts and find each ending balance.
  • Prepare the unadjusted trial balance at November 30.
