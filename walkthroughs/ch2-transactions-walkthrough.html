Meet Bryson City Bike Works

Bryson City Bike Works rents mountain bikes and leads guided trail tours out of a shop near the Tsali trail system. Below are eight transactions from the company's first month in business, this November. For each stop: think through the accounting equation effect first, jot down your own answer, then reveal the solution before moving to the journal entry for that same transaction.
